# Health Access Program

We help people get medical care, medicine and health insurance.

## Free clinic
Our partner, the Riverside Free Clinic, offers free primary care to people without insurance every Tuesday and Thursday from 9am to 4pm at 300 Pine Street, San Marcos. Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are faster.

## Prescription help
We can pay for up to 30 days of a prescription, up to $200, while we help you find a long-term option such as a discount program or insurance. This includes insulin, inhalers and blood pressure medicine.

## Dental and vision
Free dental cleanings and fillings are offered once a month through a partner dental school. Free eye exams and glasses are available for children through the school program.

## Health insurance
Our navigators help you apply for Medicaid, CHIP for children, or a marketplace plan. Appointments are available every weekday.

## Emergencies
For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Hospitals must treat emergencies even if you cannot pay. We can help with the hospital bill afterwards by applying for the hospital's charity care program.

## Transportation
Free rides to medical appointments can be booked 2 days in advance by calling (512) 555-0100.
