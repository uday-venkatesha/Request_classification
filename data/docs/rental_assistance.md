# Rent and Eviction Prevention Program

This program helps people stay in their homes when they cannot pay rent.

## What we cover
One-time help of up to $1,500 toward past-due rent, paid directly to the landlord. In special cases we can also help with a security deposit for a new home, up to $1,000. Utility bills (electric and water) can be covered up to $300 to prevent a shut-off.

## Who qualifies
Tenants living in our five service cities who have a lease in their name and have had a loss of income in the last 6 months, such as job loss, reduced hours or a medical emergency. Each household can receive rental help once every 12 months.

## How to apply
Submit a request with your lease, the past-due notice or eviction notice, and proof of income loss. A case worker will contact you within 3 business days, or within 1 business day if you already have an eviction court date.

## If you have an eviction notice
Do not ignore it. In Texas, a landlord must go to court before you can be legally removed. If you have a court date, contact our Legal Aid Clinic right away; a volunteer attorney may be able to attend the hearing with you. Changing the locks or removing your belongings without a court order is illegal in most cases.
