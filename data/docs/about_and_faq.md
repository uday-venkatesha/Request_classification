# About Bluebonnet Community Aid and General FAQ

Bluebonnet Community Aid is a nonprofit serving five cities in Central Texas: Austin, Round Rock, San Marcos, Georgetown and Pflugerville. We help with food, shelter, medical access, legal aid, education and mental health.

## How do I ask for help?
You can submit a request on our website form, call our helpline at (512) 555-0100 (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm), or walk in to our main office at 410 Oak Street, Austin, Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm.

## Who can get help?
Anyone living in our five service cities. You do not need to be a US citizen and we never ask about immigration status. Requests from outside our service area are referred to partner organizations.

## What documents do I need?
For most programs, a photo ID and proof of address (a utility bill, lease or a letter from a shelter). If you do not have documents, a case worker can still help you, so please contact us anyway.

## How long does it take?
Urgent requests (safety risk, no food, no place to sleep tonight) are reviewed the same day. Other requests are answered within 3 business days.

## Is my information private?
Yes. We only share your information with the volunteer or partner assigned to your case, and only what they need. You can ask us to delete your records at any time.

## Languages
Our staff speak English and Spanish. Interpreters for other languages are available by phone at no cost.
