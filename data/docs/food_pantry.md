# Food Pantry Program

The Bluebonnet Food Pantry gives free groceries to individuals and families who need food.

## Locations and hours
- Austin pantry, 410 Oak Street: Tuesday and Thursday 10am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 1pm.
- Round Rock pantry, 88 Mesa Drive: Wednesday 12pm to 6pm.
- San Marcos pantry, 15 River Road: Friday 10am to 4pm.
Georgetown and Pflugerville residents can use any pantry or ask for home delivery.

## How often can I visit?
Each household can visit once every 7 days. In an emergency a case worker can approve an extra visit.

## What do I get?
About 3 days of food per person in the household: canned goods, rice, pasta, fresh produce when available, milk and eggs. Baby formula and diapers are available on request while supplies last.

## Eligibility
Households with income below 200% of the federal poverty level, or anyone facing an emergency such as job loss. Bring a photo ID and proof of address on your first visit. No documents are needed for an emergency visit.

## Home delivery
Seniors over 65, people with disabilities, and people without transportation can get groceries delivered once every two weeks. Call (512) 555-0100 to sign up.

## Hot meals
Free hot meals are served at the Austin location every weekday from 12pm to 1:30pm. No registration needed.
