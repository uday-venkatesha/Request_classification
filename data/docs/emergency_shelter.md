# Emergency Shelter Program

We help people who have no safe place to sleep find emergency shelter.

## Shelter beds
Bluebonnet runs the Harbor House shelter in Austin with 40 beds for adults and 12 family rooms. Check-in is from 5pm to 9pm every day. We also have agreements with partner shelters in Round Rock and San Marcos.

## How to get a bed
Call the helpline at (512) 555-0100 or come to Harbor House at 22 Elm Avenue, Austin, before 9pm. Beds are given first to families with children, pregnant women, seniors and people with disabilities. If no bed is available, staff will call partner shelters for you.

## How long can I stay?
Up to 30 days. A case worker meets every guest within 3 days to make a housing plan. Stays can be extended by 30 days if the guest is actively working on the plan.

## Rules
Quiet hours are 10pm to 6am. No alcohol, drugs or weapons. Guests must leave the shelter between 8am and 5pm unless they are sick, but the day center at 410 Oak Street is open during that time.

## Pets
Small pets are allowed in family rooms only. Service animals are always allowed.

## Hotel vouchers
When all shelters are full, families with children under 5 may receive a hotel voucher for up to 3 nights.
