# Education Support Program

We help children and adults with school and learning.

## Free tutoring
Volunteer tutors help students from kindergarten to grade 12 in math, reading and science. Sessions are held Monday to Thursday from 4pm to 7pm at the Austin and Round Rock offices, and online. Each student gets two 1-hour sessions per week.

## GED classes
Free GED preparation classes for adults run in 12-week terms starting in January, May and September. Classes meet Tuesday and Thursday evenings. We also pay the GED test fee for students who finish the course.

## School supplies
Every August we give free backpacks with school supplies to children in grades K to 12. Families can sign up from June 1 to July 31. School uniforms can be provided in emergencies.

## English classes
Free English as a Second Language (ESL) classes for adults meet Saturday mornings from 9am to 12pm.

## Job training
A 6-week computer skills and job readiness course is offered four times a year, including help with resumes and interviews.

## Scholarships
Small scholarships of up to $500 are available for community college students living in our service area. Applications are due April 15.
