# Legal Aid Clinic

Free legal advice from volunteer attorneys for people who cannot afford a lawyer.

## What we help with
- Tenant problems: evictions, repairs, lock-outs, security deposits
- Immigration: green card renewals, DACA renewals, finding a lawyer for removal cases
- Family law: custody, child support, protective orders
- Consumer problems: debt collection, scams, wage theft

## What we do not handle
Criminal cases (we refer you to the public defender's office), business disputes, and personal injury lawsuits.

## Clinic hours
Walk-in clinic every Wednesday from 4pm to 8pm at 410 Oak Street, Austin. Appointments are available on Mondays and Fridays. Spanish-speaking attorneys are available at every clinic.

## Urgent legal help
If you have a court date within 7 days, or a family member has been detained by immigration authorities, call (512) 555-0100 and ask for the urgent legal line. We will try to connect you with an attorney within 24 hours.

## Protective orders
If you are in danger from a partner or family member, call 911 first. Our attorneys can help you file for a protective order at no cost.

## What to bring
Any papers related to your problem: court notices, letters, your lease, pay stubs or immigration documents.
