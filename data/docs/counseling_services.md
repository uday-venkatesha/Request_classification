# Counseling and Mental Health Services

We offer free and confidential mental health support.

## If you are in crisis
If you are thinking about suicide or are in immediate danger, call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) at any time, or call 911. Our staff can also connect you to crisis support during office hours.

## Free counseling
Licensed counselors offer up to 12 free sessions per person, in person or by video. Sessions are 50 minutes. We help with anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, stress and family problems. Counseling is available in English and Spanish.

## How to start
Submit a request or call (512) 555-0100. A counselor will contact you within 5 business days for a first appointment. Urgent requests are seen sooner.

## Support groups
Free weekly support groups meet at 410 Oak Street, Austin:
- Grief support: Mondays 6pm to 7:30pm
- Parents under stress: Wednesdays 6pm to 7:30pm
- Veterans group: Thursdays 6pm to 7:30pm

## Children and teens
Counselors trained to work with children and teens are available after school hours. A parent or guardian must sign the consent form for anyone under 18.

## Medication
Our counselors cannot prescribe medication. If you need medication, we refer you to the Riverside Free Clinic or a partner psychiatrist.
